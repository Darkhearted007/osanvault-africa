# Nigeria Legal Registration Guide

## ÒsánVault Africa - Regulatory Compliance Path

**Last Updated:** May 2026
**Primary Jurisdiction:** Nigeria (SEC ARIP Sandbox Pathway)

## Overview

ÒsánVault Africa requires registration under Nigerian law to operate as a Digital Asset Offering Platform (DAOP). The primary regulatory pathway is through the Nigeria Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) ARIP Sandbox program.

---

## Required Registrations

### 1. SCUML Registration (Priority)

**What:** Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering registration
**Authority:** Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU)
**Required:** Yes - mandatory for all VASPs and property investment platforms

#### Requirements
- CAC registration documents
- Company memorandum & articles
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) policy document
- Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures
- Compliance officer appointment
- Training records for staff
- Proof of identity for directors
- Proof of address for company

#### Estimated Timeline
- **Filing:** 2-3 weeks
- **Processing:** 4-6 weeks
- **Total:** 6-8 weeks

#### Fees
- Processing fee: ₦50,000 - ₦100,000
- Registration certificate: ₦25,000

#### Documents Needed
1. CAC Certificate (Certificate of Incorporation)
2. CAC Form 1 (Particulars of Company)
3. Company Audited Financials (if applicable)
4. AML/CFT Policy Document
5. Risk Assessment Report
6. Board Resolution authorizing registration

---

### 2. SEC DAOP Registration (Primary)

**What:** Digital Asset Offering Platform registration
**Authority:** Securities and Exchange Commission (Nigeria)
**Required:** Yes - mandatory under SEC regulations

#### ARIP Sandbox Pathway

The SEC's Accelerated Regulatory Innovation Program (ARIP) provides a pathway for fintech companies.

**Phase 1: Pre-Sandbox (3 months)**
- Initial application and documentation review
- Compliance framework assessment
- Technical infrastructure review

**Phase 2: Sandbox (6-12 months)**
- Limited pilot with selected users
- Real-time monitoring by SEC
- Regular reporting requirements

**Phase 3: Full Registration**
- Sandbox completion report
- Compliance certification
- Full operational approval

#### Requirements
- SCUML registration (must be obtained first)
- Business plan with risk assessment
- Smart contract audit reports
- Technical architecture documentation
- Custody and asset management procedures
- Investor protection measures
- Dispute resolution mechanism

#### Documents Needed
1. SEC ARIP Application Form
2. Business plan and model
3. Technical whitepaper
4. Smart contract security audit
5. Legal opinion letter
6. AML/KYC framework
7. Fidelity bond proof (required)

---

### 3. Fidelity Bond

**What:** Financial guarantee to protect investors
**Required:** Yes - SEC mandates for DAOPs
**Amount:** Based on projected investor funds (typically ₦50-100M)

#### Options
1. **Bank Guarantee:** Most common, easier to obtain
2. **Insurance Bond:** Requires specific VASP coverage
3. **Cash Deposit:** Place funds in escrow account
